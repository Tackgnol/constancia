# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Game Masters preparing and running tabletop RPG campaigns, plus players maintaining the character dossiers and private campaign knowledge shared with them. GMs work in a dense live-session control surface; players use focused, permission-safe views linked to their Discord identity.

## Product Purpose

Constancia connects a GM war room, a Discord bot, and player-facing dossiers to one canonical campaign state. It should reduce live-session friction, keep game-system logic consistent, and let information move deliberately between private GM preparation, Discord play, and player records.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, nocturnal, and precise. The voice is concise and operational, with enough atmosphere to support horror and dark-fantasy play without obscuring the task.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, soft social-media styling, ornamental fantasy interfaces, rainbow status systems, and confirmation-heavy workflows that interrupt live play. Atmosphere must not come at the cost of legibility or familiar controls.

## Design Principles

- Put the live tabletop task before decoration.
- Keep dense information scannable through hierarchy, grouping, and consistent system colors.
- Make permissions and audience boundaries visible in the interface and enforce them in the architecture.
- Preserve campaign continuity by using one canonical backend state across GM, player, and bot surfaces.
- Use atmosphere as a quiet framing device, not as an interaction tax.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, keyboard operation, form labeling, focus visibility, and status feedback. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never rely on color alone to communicate state.
