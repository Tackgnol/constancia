# Constancia — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary:** Game Masters running VTM V5 sessions. They're at a laptop during or between sessions — managing narrative flow, firing events at players, tracking NPCs and faction health. They are the director of the experience. The interface should feel like it was built _for them_, not for a generic SaaS customer.

**Future (out of MVP scope):** Players accessing a journal view, but this is not a design concern yet.

**Job to be done:** Orchestrate a live or async TTRPG session with precision — queue events, fire them at the right moment, watch player outcomes, log narrative beats. High information density, but never chaotic.

### Brand Personality

**Mystery. Intrigue. Cinematic.**

The platform is a tool, but it should _feel_ like an artifact. Not robotic — calculated. The silence before the kill. An expensive leather-bound ledger retrofitted with cold surveillance logic. Every interaction should feel like a move in a high-stakes shadow war.

### Aesthetic Direction

**The Sovereign Grimoire** (per DESIGN.md — this is the canonical reference).

- **Color palette:** Warm near-blacks (not pure #000), oxblood red (`#800000`) as primary, ivory (`#e2e2e6`) for body text, gold-grey as tertiary. No cyan, no purple, no "developer dark mode" blues.
- **Typography:** Newsreader (display/headings — the grimoire weight) + IBM Plex Sans (body/narration) + IBM Plex Mono (data/labels/timestamps). Type hierarchy replaces color-coding.
- **Surfaces:** 4-level tonal depth. No 1px borders — boundaries come from background shifts (the "No-Line" rule).
- **Corners:** Sharp (0px or 0.125rem max). This is a war room, not a social app.
- **Active state:** Candle-glow radial using `#ff8371` at 5% — warm, uneven light behind the focused element.
- **Texture:** Subtle SVG film grain on the base canvas to prevent digital banding and maintain cinematic atmosphere.

**References:** Cinematic war room dashboards, high-end editorial design, premium leather goods, tactical operations software.

**Anti-references:**

- Roll20 (gamey, cluttered, skeuomorphic in all the wrong ways)
- Generic SaaS dashboards (hero metrics, rounded cards, teal/purple gradients)
- "Developer dark mode" (cyan on black, purple accents, glassmorphism as a style choice)

### Design Principles

1. **Functional without robotic.** Every element earns its place — but the cumulative effect should feel like a crafted instrument, not a CRUD form.
2. **No-Line architecture.** Depth is created by tonal shifts and light (candle-glow), never by drawing lines. Borders are noise.
3. **Type does the work.** Hierarchy lives in font weight, size, and family — not color variety. Newsreader carries drama; Mono carries precision.
4. **Whisper-quiet until action.** The resting state is calm and controlled. Moments of interaction (firing an event, a bestial failure) should feel significant — not flashy.
5. **The GM is the director.** The interface defers to the GM's narrative authority. No affordance should feel like it's bossing them around.
